### Overview

Contributed to **Payra SAMS** (Staff and Access Management System), a government-grade access control system developed for the **Payra Port Authority**, enabling secure biometric-based access control for a critical national infrastructure organization.

### Key Contributions

- **Configurable Notification System:** Designed and developed a configurable multi-channel notification system supporting **SMS**, **browser push notifications**, and **in-app notifications** via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).

- **Automated Notification Triggers:** Implemented automated notifications for employee access requests, visitor approvals, biometric verification outcomes, application status updates, and unauthorized access events.

- **Business Logic & Approval Workflows:** Developed the full business logic for access applications, including multi-step approval workflows, biometric device integration (**ZKTeco Push SDK**), and secure document management with **MinIO**.
