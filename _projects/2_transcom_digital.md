### Overview

Contributed to **Transcom Digital**, one of Bangladesh's leading premium multi-brand electronics e-commerce platforms, by maintaining and enhancing its distributed microservice backend.

### Key Contributions

- **Payment Refund Integration:** Designed and implemented the Payment Refund feature by integrating the **bKash Refund API**, enabling administrators to process refunds directly from the platform admin portal.

- **Microservice Maintenance:** Maintained and enhanced **10 microservices**, resolving critical production issues and improving platform reliability and uptime.

- **ERP Synchronization:** Investigated and resolved critical product pricing synchronization issues from the ERP system during scheduled jobs, and contributed to ERP API integrations.

- **Security Hardening:** Strengthened platform security by implementing **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** for the admin portal and improving authentication mechanisms for internal service communications.

- **Database Migration:** Successfully migrated **PostgreSQL from version 13 to 17**, improving database performance and reducing cloud infrastructure costs by approximately **USD 10-20 per day**.
