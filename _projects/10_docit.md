### Overview

Contributed to **DocIT**, a cloud-based learning management system (LMS) designed for healthcare organizations to create customized training modules and deliver pre-built compliance courses to their staff.

### Key Contributions

- **Microservice Maintenance:** Maintained and monitored **13 microservices** on the backend, ensuring system stability and resolving issues across user management, profile, payment, email, and storage services.

- **Feature Development:** Developed key platform features including course management, organization creation workflows, and progress tracking and reporting dashboards.

- **Multi-Tenancy Migration:** Assisted in converting the project from a **single-tenant** to a **multi-tenant architecture**, implementing proper data isolation strategies and organization-level access controls.
