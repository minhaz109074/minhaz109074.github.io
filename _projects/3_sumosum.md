### Overview

Contributed to **SumoSum**, an AI-powered business forecasting platform that automates financial planning and generates multi-year business projections from natural language inputs.

### Key Contributions

- **AI-Driven Workflow Design:** Designed and implemented an AI-driven workflow that transforms free-text business ideas and **Business Model Canvas (BMC)** inputs into structured financial data using the **OpenAI GPT-5 mini** model.

- **JSON-to-Financial Data Conversion:** Developed a feature converting AI-generated structured JSON responses into financial planning data, automatically populating multiple forecasting modules across the platform.

- **Automated 5-Year Forecasting:** Automated the generation of five-year business forecasts — including revenue, operational costs, staffing, logistics, product sales, taxation, and financing estimates — reducing manual data preparation time by **over 90%**.
