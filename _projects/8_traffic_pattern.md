### Overview

Participated in **Bangladesh's first international AI traffic detection competition**, applying state-of-the-art object detection models to analyze real-world traffic patterns in Dhaka.

### Key Contributions

- **Multi-Model Evaluation:** Trained and benchmarked multiple object detection architectures (including YOLO variants) on a complex, real-world Dhaka traffic dataset containing dense vehicle overlaps, diverse weather conditions, and mixed vehicle types.

- **Challenging Dataset:** Handled overlapping and partially occluded vehicle detection — a significant challenge in dense urban traffic environments where standard bounding box prediction breaks down.

- **Performance Analysis:** Systematically evaluated models across mAP metrics for various vehicle categories, contributing insights on model robustness in South Asian traffic scenarios.

- **Competition Submission:** Submitted a strong performing entry to the DhakaAI challenge, showcasing applied deep learning for traffic analytics in resource-constrained environments.
